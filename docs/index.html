Just what you need

Pick. Open. Carry on.

01

Quick by default

A native picker beside your pointer. Click a destination, press its shortcut, or copy the link with ⌘C. Start at login keeps it ready.

02

Your rules, locally

Route links by domain, path or source app. Choose a fallback for everything else. Your settings stay on your Mac.

03

More than browsers

Add applications as destinations, including Zoom for supported meeting invitations. No browser-data access, Accessibility or Automation permission needed.

Private by design

Your links aren't
our business.

Pickosaurus sends no personal data to the developer. No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or app-provided crash reports.

Small, efficient and built around standard macOS APIs. Links are handled locally and passed to the destination you choose.

Manual updates and website hosting use GitHub. Read the short privacy policy for those disclosures.
